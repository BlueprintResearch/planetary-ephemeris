Consolidated planetary ephemeris library with 45 exported functions. One import gives you geocentric/heliocentric longitude, latitude, declination, right ascension, speed, distance, aspects, midpoints, zodiac signs, phase angles, synodic cycles, parallels, sidereal time, Ascendant/Midheaven, house cusps, and lunar node positions for all 10 solar system bodies, plus Earth for heliocentric charts.


█ [b]ACCURACY — VALIDATED AGAINST JPL DE440[/b]

Every body was validated against NASA's DE440 ephemeris (via Skyfield) across [b]250 years (1850–2100)[/b]. Using only [b]1.6% of the full VSOP87D theory[/b] (511 of 31,577 terms), this library achieves sub-arcminute accuracy for all planets:

  [b]Sun[/b]         0.004°  (14 arcseconds)
  [b]Mercury[/b]     0.005°  (18")
  [b]Venus[/b]       0.006°  (22")
  [b]Mars[/b]        0.010°  (36")
  [b]Jupiter[/b]     0.007°  (25")
  [b]Saturn[/b]      0.009°  (32")
  [b]Uranus[/b]      0.013°  (47")
  [b]Neptune[/b]     0.017°  (61")
  [b]Moon[/b]        0.062°  (3.7')
  [b]Pluto[/b]       0.059°  (3.5')

All bodies under 0.1° RMS — more than sufficient for aspect calculations, ingress timing, and planetary line work. The Sun is accurate to 14 arcseconds using a truncated series that fits entirely inside Pine Script's token limits.

[b]New calculation accuracy (validated over 250 years):[/b]
  Latitude:      0.0001°–0.093° RMS (all planets < 0.1°)
  Right Ascension: 0.006°–0.062° RMS (all planets < 0.1°)
  Helio Declination: 0.005°–0.028° RMS
  Distance:      0.00001–0.006 AU RMS (inner planets excellent)
  GMST:          0.112° RMS
  Ascendant/MC:  0.021° RMS


█ [b]WHAT'S NEW (V2)[/b]

The original ephemeris required 11 chained library imports. A full validation audit uncovered critical coefficient errors and motivated this rewrite:

• [b]L1[0] Precession Fix[/b] — All 8 VSOP87 planets had incorrect longitude rate coefficients (VSOP87B values instead of VSOP87D). Each was missing +0.24382 rad/millennium of general precession. This single correction reduced error from ~0.75° to < 0.1° across the board.

• [b]28% Smaller[/b] — 4,300 lines across 11 files → ~3,100 lines in 1 file.

• [b]Single Import[/b] — No dependency chain. Faster execution.

• [b]Moon Improvements[/b] — Functions accept raw `time` directly. Node functions renamed with explicit north/south designation.

• [b]24 New Functions[/b] — Latitude, heliocentric declination, right ascension, distance, obliquity, elongation, aspect angle, midpoint, zodiac signs, phase angle, illumination, synodic phase, parallel/contraparallel, speed differential, sidereal time, Ascendant/Descendant/Midheaven/IC, and house cusps. All validated against DE440 over 250 years.


█ [b]THEORIES[/b]

[b]VSOP87D[/b] (Bretagnon & Francou, 1988) — Mercury through Neptune
  511 truncated terms out of 31,577 total (1.6%). Heliocentric spherical
  coordinates in the ecliptic of date.

[b]ELP2000-82[/b] (Chapront-Touzé & Chapront, 1983) — Moon
  91 terms (48 longitude + 43 latitude) from Meeus Chapter 47.

[b]Meeus Series[/b] (Meeus, 1998) — Pluto
  Analytical series from "Astronomical Algorithms" Ch. 37.
  Valid ±1 century from J2000.


█ [b]HOW TO USE[/b]

Import the library:
[pine]
import BlueprintResearch/blueprint_ephemeris_lib/1 as eph
[/pine]

[b]Basic — plot a planet's geocentric longitude and declination:[/b]
[pine]
float jupiter_lon = eph.get_longitude(eph.Planet.Jupiter, time, true)
float jupiter_decl = eph.get_declination(eph.Planet.Jupiter, time)

plot(jupiter_lon, "Jupiter Geo Lon", color.yellow)
plot(jupiter_decl, "Jupiter Decl", color.red)
[/pine]

[b]Retrograde detection:[/b]
[pine]
bool mercury_retro = eph.is_retrograde(eph.Planet.Mercury, time)
bgcolor(mercury_retro ? color.new(color.red, 90) : na)
[/pine]

[b]Latitude, Right Ascension & Heliocentric Declination:[/b]
[pine]
float mars_lat = eph.get_latitude(eph.Planet.Mars, time, true)
float mars_ra = eph.get_right_ascension(eph.Planet.Mars, time)
float mars_hdecl = eph.get_helio_declination(eph.Planet.Mars, time)

plot(mars_lat, "Mars Geo Latitude", color.orange)
plot(mars_ra, "Mars RA", color.yellow)
plot(mars_hdecl, "Mars Helio Declination", color.red)
[/pine]

[b]Aspect detection with orb checking:[/b]
[pine]
float angle = eph.get_aspect_angle(eph.Planet.Jupiter, eph.Planet.Saturn, time, true)

float orb = 8.0
bool is_conjunction = angle < orb
bool is_square = math.abs(angle - 90) < orb
bool is_trine = math.abs(angle - 120) < orb
bool is_opposition = math.abs(angle - 180) < orb

bgcolor(is_conjunction ? color.new(color.yellow, 85) : is_opposition ? color.new(color.red, 85) : is_trine ? color.new(color.green, 85) : is_square ? color.new(color.orange, 85) : na)
plot(angle, "Jupiter-Saturn Aspect", color.white)
[/pine]

[b]Heliocentric synodic cycle (Earth-Jupiter):[/b]
[pine]
float helio_phase = eph.get_synodic_phase(eph.Planet.Earth, eph.Planet.Jupiter, time, false)

plot(helio_phase, "Earth-Jupiter Synodic Phase", color.yellow)
hline(0, "Conjunction (0°)", color.gray)
hline(180, "Opposition (180°)", color.gray)
[/pine]

[b]Elongation — angular distance from the Sun:[/b]
[pine]
float venus_elong = eph.get_elongation(eph.Planet.Venus, time)
float mercury_elong = eph.get_elongation(eph.Planet.Mercury, time)

plot(venus_elong, "Venus Elongation", color.yellow)
plot(mercury_elong, "Mercury Elongation", color.orange)
// Venus maxes out around 47 deg, Mercury around 28 deg
[/pine]

[b]Signed elongation — morning star vs evening star:[/b]
[pine]
float venus_elong = eph.get_signed_elongation(eph.Planet.Venus, time)
bool is_morning = venus_elong > 0
color elong_color = is_morning ? color.yellow : color.blue

plot(venus_elong, "Venus Signed Elongation", elong_color)
hline(0, "Sun", color.gray)
// Above zero = morning star (west), below zero = evening star (east)
[/pine]

[b]Ascendant & Midheaven (requires your location):[/b]
[pine]
float lat = input.float(40.7, "Latitude")
float lon = input.float(-74.0, "Longitude")

[ASC, DSC, MC, IC] = eph.get_angles(time, lat, lon)

plot(ASC, "Ascendant", color.green)
plot(MC, "Midheaven", color.yellow)
plot(DSC, "Descendant", color.red)
plot(IC, "IC", color.purple)
[/pine]

[b]Moon nodes and declination:[/b]
[pine]
float north_node = eph.get_mean_north_node_lon(time)
float south_node = eph.get_mean_south_node_lon(time)
float moon_decl = eph.get_declination(time)

plot(north_node, "North Node", color.green)
plot(south_node, "South Node", color.purple)
plot(moon_decl, "Moon Declination", color.orange)
[/pine]

[b]Dynamic planet selection from input:[/b]
[pine]
string planet_str = input.string("Sun", "Planet", options=["Sun", "Moon", "Mercury", "Venus", "Mars", "Jupiter", "Saturn", "Uranus", "Neptune", "Pluto"])
eph.Planet p = eph.string_to_planet(planet_str)

float geo = eph.get_longitude(p, time, true)
float helio = eph.get_longitude(p, time, false)
float speed = eph.get_speed(p, time)

plot(geo, "Geocentric", color.yellow)
plot(helio, "Heliocentric", color.blue)
plot(speed * 100, "Speed x100", color.white)
[/pine]

All functions accept TradingView's `time` variable directly.


█ [b]FUNCTIONS[/b]

[b]Core API[/b] (all planets):
  `get_longitude(Planet, time, preferGeo)` — geo or heliocentric longitude (deg)
  `get_latitude(Planet, time, preferGeo)` — geo or heliocentric ecliptic latitude (deg)
  `get_declination(Planet, time)` — equatorial declination (deg)
  `get_helio_declination(Planet, time)` — declination as seen from the Sun (deg)
  `get_right_ascension(Planet, time)` — equatorial right ascension (deg)
  `get_speed(Planet, time)` — longitude speed (deg/day)
  `get_radius(Planet, time)` — heliocentric distance from Sun (AU)
  `get_distance(Planet, time)` — geocentric distance from Earth (AU)
  `is_retrograde(Planet, time)` — true when retrograde
  `string_to_planet(string)` — name to Planet enum
  `normalizeLongitude(float)` — normalize to [0, 360)

[b]Aspects & Relationships[/b] (two planets):
  `get_aspect_angle(Planet, Planet, time, preferGeo)` — shortest arc 0–180 deg
  `get_midpoint(Planet, Planet, time, preferGeo)` — zodiacal midpoint (handles wraparound)
  `get_synodic_phase(Planet, Planet, time, preferGeo)` — phase within synodic cycle 0–360 deg
      For heliocentric synodic cycles, use Planet.Earth (e.g., Earth–Jupiter instead of Sun–Jupiter).
  `get_speed_diff(Planet, Planet, time)` — speed difference (deg/day)
  `is_parallel(Planet, Planet, time, orb)` — same declination within orb
  `is_contraparallel(Planet, Planet, time, orb)` — opposite declination within orb

[b]Visibility & Phase[/b]:
  `get_elongation(Planet, time)` — angular separation from Sun 0–180 deg
  `get_signed_elongation(Planet, time)` — signed elongation: positive = west/morning star, negative = east/evening star
  `get_phase_angle(Planet, time)` — Sun-Planet-Earth angle (illumination geometry)
  `get_illuminated_fraction(Planet, time)` — fraction of disk lit 0.0–1.0

[b]Zodiac[/b]:
  `get_sign(longitude)` — sign index 0–11 (Aries=0, Pisces=11)
  `get_sign_degree(longitude)` — degrees within current sign 0–30
  `get_sign_name(longitude)` — sign name string ("Aries" through "Pisces")

[b]Obliquity[/b]:
  `get_mean_obliquity(time)` — mean obliquity of the ecliptic (deg)

[b]Sidereal Time & Angles[/b] (location-based):
  `get_gmst(time)` — Greenwich Mean Sidereal Time (deg)
  `get_lst(time, observer_lon)` — Local Sidereal Time (deg)
  `get_angles(time, lat, lon)` — returns [ASC, DSC, MC, IC] (four angular cusps, deg)
  `get_house_cusp_whole_sign(asc_lon, house_num)` — Whole Sign house cusp (deg)
  `get_house_cusp_equal(asc_lon, house_num)` — Equal House cusp (deg)

[b]Averages[/b]:
  `get_avg6_geo_lon` / `get_avg6_helio_lon` — Mercury–Saturn average
  `get_avg8_geo_lon` / `get_avg8_helio_lon` — Mercury–Neptune average

[b]Moon[/b] (direct access):
  `get_geo_ecl_lon(time)` · `get_geo_ecl_lat(time)` · `get_obliquity_j(time)`
  `get_declination(time)` (Moon overload)
  `get_mean_north_node_lon(time)` · `get_mean_south_node_lon(time)`
  `get_true_north_node_lon(time)` · `get_true_south_node_lon(time)`
  `get_north_node_declination(time)` · `get_south_node_declination(time)`

█ [b]LIMITATIONS[/b]

• Truncated series — sub-degree accuracy, not sub-arcsecond. More than sufficient for ingress timing and aspect work.
• Validated against DE440 across 250 years (1850–2100). Over this full span, the worst-case VSOP87 planet (Uranus) is 0.017° RMS / 0.043° max error. Ingress dates manually verified back to the late 1800s with consistent accuracy.
• Pluto uses Meeus series, limited to ±1 century from J2000.
• Moon has no speed function.
• Location-dependent functions (ASC, MC, houses, LST) require geographic latitude and longitude as inputs. These change with Earth's rotation (~1° per 4 minutes), so they are most meaningful on intraday charts.
• Mercury heliocentric latitude has higher error (~0.23° RMS) due to its large orbital inclination and limited truncated B-series terms. Geocentric latitude is fine (0.09°).
